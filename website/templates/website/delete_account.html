FlexyRide Account Deletion

Learn how to delete your FlexyRide account and managed personal data for both Passenger and Driver apps, published by Patmac Tech.

Option 1: Request Deletion Directly In-App (Recommended)

You can request account deletion at any time directly within the FlexyRide Passenger or FlexyRide Driver mobile app:

  1. Open the FlexyRide mobile app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap on Settings and select Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your deletion request.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the mobile application, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing our Support & Privacy team:

To: support@flexyridegh.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]

Please send the request from the email address associated with your FlexyRide account so we can verify your identity.

Data Retention & Deletion Details

  • Data Deleted Immediately: Your profile info (name, email, phone number), active login sessions, saved places, and push notification tokens.
  • Data Retained (if required by law): Completed ride financial transactions and tax invoices are retained for up to 5 years strictly for statutory accounting, audit, and tax compliance as mandated by local laws.
